The basis attribution is an accounting decomposition of the futures mark-to-market
move relative to the same-family spot close; it is not a claim that the basis leg is
risk-free. The large negative spot beta in some families is why this remains a long
equity-beta strategy.

The raw ETF benchmark generated by `download_etf_benchmark.py` returned 1,004 dates and
ended at 8.32% cumulative return over the full snapshot. It is deliberately labeled raw close and excludes
cash distributions; a reinvested-distribution series should replace it before making a
total-return claim.
